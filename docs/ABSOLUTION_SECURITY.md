# Absolution attack restrictions

While Absolution is active, both the Stand and its summoner receive pacifism and no-throwing restrictions. Absolution also cancels item and ranged attack chains, Arrow Stand melee has an explicit pacifism guard, harmful custom Stand powers check pacifism directly, and grenade-family explosives refuse to arm for pacified users.

Player-facing feedback is intentionally plain: Absolution states that attacks are unavailable while the barrier is active and that the effect has ended when recalled.
