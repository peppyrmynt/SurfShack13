The preview copies the game's rules: limbs tween into each keyframe's pose over that frame's duration, snap when their facing changes, and the whole body returns to normal once the last frame ends. Tweens are close to BYOND's but can differ a little in game. When facing east or west, both arms draw over the torso, the same as in game.
